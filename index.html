Privacy Policy

App: My Planner (package: com.orderlymatters.my_planner)
Developer: Orderly Matters
Contact: orderlymatters2026@gmail.com
Effective date: November 23, 2026

My Planner is a planner app that combines a calendar, tasks, notes (typed and handwritten) and projects. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information the app accesses

  • Google account information. If you sign in with Google, the app receives your name, email address and profile photo. The photo is downloaded once and cached on your device.
  • Calendar data. With your permission, the app reads and writes events on your device's calendar provider (including Google Calendar events synced to your phone). It does this to show your events, create events for dated tasks, edit or delete events you choose, and add guests to events you create. You choose which calendars the app displays.
  • Content you create. Tasks, sub-tasks, notes, handwriting, typed text, projects, tags, folders, and any photos or stickers you add to notes.
  • Contacts (email address only). When you add an event guest, the app uses the system contact picker to receive the single email address you select. The app does not read your full contact list.

2. How the information is used

We use this information only to provide the app's features: showing and editing your calendar, tasks and notes, and syncing your content across your own devices. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. Storage and sync

  • Your content is stored on your device.
  • If you sign in, your tasks, notes, note pages (including handwriting), projects, tags, folders and photos are also synced to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) so they appear on your other devices. Each user's data is stored under their own account and is accessible only to that signed-in user through security rules.
  • Event guest information is stored in your phone's calendar only, not in our sync database.
  • App settings (theme, visible calendars, widget options, pen preferences) stay on each device and are not synced.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for their own purposes. Your data is processed by Google (Firebase and Google Sign-In) solely to run the app's sign-in and sync, under Google's terms and privacy policy. When you invite guests to a calendar event, the event details are sent to those guests by Google Calendar.

5. Google API Services disclosure

My Planner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the app's user-facing features, is not transferred to others except as needed to provide those features or to comply with law, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans except with your consent, for security purposes, or as required by law.

6. Advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising. The app does not include third-party analytics or tracking.

7. Security

Data is transmitted to Firebase over encrypted connections, and access to synced data is restricted to the signed-in account that owns it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and data deletion

  • You can use the app without signing in; in that case nothing is synced to the cloud.
  • You can revoke calendar permission at any time in your device's Settings.
  • You can sign out in the app's Settings at any time.
  • To request deletion of your synced data stored in our cloud database, email orderlymatters2026@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in. We will delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.
  • You can also remove the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do, and we will post the new version at this same address.
